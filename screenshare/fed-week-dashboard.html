Strait of Hormuz

~1 in 5
barrels of the world's oil transit a 2-mile-wide lane. Vessel traffic has collapsed to ~30–35% of pre-conflict levels. Tankers reroute the long way — slower transit means less supply arriving on time.

The pipeline lag — "rockets and feathers"

Crude → refinery → terminal → your corner station takes 2–4 weeks. The sign today is pricing oil from three weeks ago. Prices rocket up on the way in and feather down on the way out — the station raises fast and lowers slow.

The through-line answer

More expensive if you BORROW. Cards 22.15%, 10-yr 4.75%, mortgages 6.66% — and three Fed officials just voted to raise, so the fall surprise skews UP.

Flat-to-better if you SAVE + INVEST. HYSAs ~4.15% vs a 0.38% national average; the index reclaimed its 50-day and sits 6.66% over the 200-day.

The three moves — no prediction required

1 · The fixed payment. $250 fixed instead of the minimum → 2.2 yrs not 19.2, keep $6,861. Card app → Payments → Autopay → Fixed → 250 → 2 days after payday.
2 · The gas gap. July fuel total vs last July (~$95/mo for 2 cars). Decide which 50/30/20 line gives it up — don't let it eat the 20.
3 · The parked cash. 0.38% → ~4.15%. Twenty minutes. The only guaranteed return on the list.

The bridge — two lanes (say the guardrail)

Lane 1 · Credit & business: fix the score → borrow cheaper → business funding when you need capital.
Lane 2 · Investing: earned income only, through the brokerage, on a schedule.
Never margin. Never borrowed money in the market. Credit money does not become investment capital.
The Fed sets the weather. Your credit score decides your house.
